CHAPTER I

Visual Philosophy

"The library is not a building — it is a breathing entity. Every corner holds a memory, and every memory can mutate into a monster."

The visual philosophy of the game relies on the principle of Beautiful Decay — where gorgeous environments slowly mutate into horrifying nightmares, yet the beauty never completely fades; it lingers like a shadow amidst the corruption.

First Principle

Living Breath

The library "breathes" and changes constantly. Slow movements, faint pulsing, expansion, and contraction — everything is in perpetual motion.

Second Principle

Gradual Decay

Everything erodes slowly. Decaying textures, fading colors, frayed edges — annihilation in aesthetic forms.

Third Principle

Duality

Beauty morphs into terror and vice versa. The exact same scene can be both mesmerizing and deeply disturbing at once.

Fourth Principle

Text as Entity

Words become physical objects. Letters crawl, pages fly like swarms, and texts bleed into reality.
